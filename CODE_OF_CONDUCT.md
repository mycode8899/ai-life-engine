# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AI Life Engine is a public developer project. People working on it are expected to be respectful, specific, and careful with other people's time and data.

Welcome behavior:

- Critique designs and patches, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask when a comment is unclear.
- Give reviewers enough context to reproduce a failure.
- Keep personal data, credentials, and private systems out of issues, logs, and tests.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, slurs, or sexual attention that was not asked for.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Asking contributors to run untrusted code against their personal machines or private data as the price of a review.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and any other space that exists to work on this project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainers in private. After a public repository exists, use the private channel listed there. Do not include exploit details or personal data in a public issue.

Maintainers will look at the report, may remove comments or commits that break this policy, and may reject further participation. They will tell the reporter what they decided, within the limits of privacy.
